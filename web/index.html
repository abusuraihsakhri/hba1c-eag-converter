Calculation method

eAG (mg/dL) = 28.7 × HbA1c (%) − 46.7 IFCC (mmol/mol) = 10.929 × (HbA1c (%) − 2.15)

Inverse conversions algebraically rearrange these equations; mg/dL is converted to mmol/L using a factor of 18.015.

References: American Diabetes Association (ADAG) and NGSP / IFCC standardization.
